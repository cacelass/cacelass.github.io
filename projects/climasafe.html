ClimaSafe · early warning · ML

Heat and cold risk warning
before the day arrives.

An early-warning system against heat and cold. Pick any point in Spain, a day and an hour, and you'll know your cardiovascular risk level: safe, caution or danger. It combines official weather data with real mortality, a calibrated model ensemble and your personal factors — age, health, medication, activity — to tell you the risk you run, not the average. Live forecast from Open-Meteo.

The human cost

3,832 deaths Summer 2025 · MoMo · May 16 – Sep 30

deaths attributable to heat in Spain. 57% occurred in August and 84% were people over 74. The second deadliest summer on record — and the hottest since 1961 (AEMET).

4,581 deaths Summer 2026 · MoMo · as of Aug 19

deaths by August 19 alone: already exceeds all of summer 2025. July (2,025) was the second deadliest month of the series. With September ahead, 2022's record (4,789) is under threat.

Since 2015, high temperatures have claimed more than 27,500 lives in Spain. Not headlines: excess mortality estimated over civil registries by the MoMo system (Instituto de Salud Carlos III).
Heat doesn't warn twice — and with climate change, every summer will be hotter and deadlier than the last.

What it solves

Heat and cold don't kill only on the day of the blow:
they wear the body down over days, and the damage shows up late.

The system crosses each province's weather with real registered mortality and computes every day's risk: safe, caution or danger.
It's an early warning: it prefers to warn you — even if sometimes wrong — over staying silent.

It doesn't stop at the population average: it adjusts the risk to your age, health, medication and activity, because a 72-year-old is not a 25-year-old runner.

And climate change isn't slowing down: every summer is hotter than the last and, with it, risky days and their human cost keep rising.

Your personalized cardiovascular risk anywhere in Spain, on the day and hour you choose.

Vulnerable groups

Care homes, day centres and families with elderly: early warnings when a day's risk matters.

Municipalities

Trigger local heat plans with province-level risk levels, not generic alerts.

03
how it works

How it works

Official data

ERA5 and AEMET for weather; MoMo (Instituto de Salud Carlos III) as the target. Public, verifiable sources — nothing opaque.

Live data

Real-time forecast from Open-Meteo: the risk is computed with today's and the coming hours' weather, not yesterday's. Refreshable every hour.

Calibrated ensemble

XGBoost for heat, RandomForest for cold and an LSTM with province embedding. The combination is a deterministic formula, not a black box:

riesgo = Σ wᵢ·pᵢ  ·  wᵢ ∝ 1/|Cᵢ|
It knows when to doubt

Conformal prediction provides confidence levels: the system warns with the calm of someone who knows what it doesn't know.

Personalized risk

Age, sex, comorbidities, medication, acclimatisation and activity adjust your risk, not a population average.

Literature-based factors

The multipliers that adjust your risk (age, comorbidities, medication, acclimatisation...) come from published, peer-reviewed epidemiology papers, with their ranges and limits documented — not from opinion.

No tricks

Date-based splits, leakage control and honest published metrics. What can be checked, is checked.
